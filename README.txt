Discover Kigali on Foot — Cloudflare Pages upload

This package contains the Discover Kigali on Foot static website and its local image assets.

Features included:
- Itinerary & Maps with Google Maps walking directions from Kigali Heights to Downtown Kigali and Kimironko Market.
- Age suitability notice: routes are not suitable for children under 10 years or adults over 70 years.
- Important Information: duration, reservation/cancellation notes, what to bring, and know-before-you-go guidance.
- About Us portrait and tour photo gallery.
- Language selector for English, French, Kinyarwanda, Kiswahili, Spanish and German. Key interface headings and controls change language; some detailed route/operational text remains in English.
- Currency selector for RWF, USD, EUR and GBP. The page tries to load daily reference rates for RWF from the Banque Nationale du Rwanda provider via Frankfurter and has approximate fallback rates. Converted amounts are estimates only; confirmed booking prices remain in RWF.
- Share button using native share when available, with copy-link/prompt fallbacks.
- “What travelers loved” highlights and experience inquiry cards for walking tours, possible car sightseeing and local market visits. Car options and other arrangements must be confirmed over WhatsApp; these are not third-party partner listings.

Customer-review figures included in this package were supplied during the website editing conversation. Please confirm their source and that they apply to your tour business before publishing them as your own ratings. The traveler-highlight cards avoid invented direct quotations.

Deploy: open your Cloudflare Pages project and upload this ZIP using Direct Upload / drag and drop. This ZIP has not been deployed automatically.
