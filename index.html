Itinerary & Maps

Choose a city walk starting at Kigali Heights. Open the walking directions for a live route, and confirm the route and timing with your guide before setting off.

Route 01 · City walk

Kigali Heights → Downtown Kigali

Begin at Kigali Heights, then walk toward Downtown Kigali to experience the city’s business streets, local shops and everyday urban life.

  1. Meet your guide at Kigali Heights.
  2. Follow the walking route toward the city centre, taking time for street scenes and photo stops.
  3. Finish in Downtown Kigali and explore the surrounding shops and public spaces.
Open walking map ↗
Route 02 · Market walk

Kigali Heights → Kimironko Market

Travel on foot from Kigali Heights toward Kimironko Market, where you can discover lively market lanes, fresh produce, everyday shopping and local trading.

  1. Meet your guide at Kigali Heights.
  2. Use the walking directions to head toward Kimironko.
  3. Explore Kimironko Market with your guide and learn about local products and market life.

Important age and suitability notice

These itineraries are not suitable for children under 10 years or adults over 70 years. Both routes involve walking through urban streets and may include busy crossings. Please check the route and conditions with your guide before departure.
